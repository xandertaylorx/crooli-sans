FONTLOG for Crooli Sans
=======================

This file records the changes made to Crooli Sans, as recommended by the
SIL Open Font License.


Basic font information
----------------------

Crooli Sans is the typeface of Crooli (https://crooli.com). It is a modified
version of Geist 1.800 (https://github.com/vercel/geist-font), released under
the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Geist has no Reserved Font Name.

Crooli Sans keeps Geist's outlines unchanged. It promotes four of Geist's own
stylistic alternates to the default forms:

  ss02  single-storey a       (the double-storey a moves to ss02)
  ss04  straight-leg R        (the Geist R moves to ss04)
  ss06  spurless G            (the Geist G moves to ss06)
  ss08  round dots            (the square dots move to ss08)


ChangeLog
---------

28 October 2026 (Crooli) Crooli Sans 1.000
- Forked from Geist 1.800: 18 static styles and 2 variable fonts.
- Promoted ss02, ss04, ss06 and ss08 to the defaults by exchanging glyph
  shapes, so outlines, advances, kerning, mark anchors and variations travel
  with each shape.
- Renamed the family to Crooli Sans, with correct style linking for all
  weights, and set the vendor ID to CROO.
- Relabelled the swapped stylistic sets.
